Foundational Umbra Lore

Kael Varyn and Morana Wentworth

Morana Wentworth was never meant for the cold wilds of the north. Born to nobility, shaped by etiquette and expectation, she entered the world as a daughter intended for political marriage.

Kael Varyn was the opposite. Mountain-forged, brutal, and carved from grief, he rose through the Umbra Pack as a weapon who learned to think before he struck.

Their worlds collided not by chance, but by consequence. Morana’s keen intelligence, adaptability, and quiet defiance drew Kael’s attention in a way nothing else had in years.

She saw the man beneath the weapon. He saw the steel beneath her softness.

Their Legacy

Sixty years after Kael Varyn claimed Morana before court and pack alike, the Umbra stand as a power beyond crowns. Greydeep Hold has grown into a fortress carved from mountain bone and wolf magic, its halls echoing with the strength of generations shaped by the two leaders who raised the Varyn name from ruin.

When Velisport fell during the mage uprisings, refugees fled north through snow and ash, expecting death. They reached Greydeep instead.
